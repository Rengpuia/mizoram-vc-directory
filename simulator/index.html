Multi-District Test Scenarios & Live Verification

  1. Test Multi-District Switching in Citizen App:
    In the Phonebook on the left, click the District badge button (top right of the header, e.g. "Kolasib"). A bottom sheet opens with all 11 Mizoram districts (Aizawl, Lunglei, Champhai, Kolasib, etc.). Select "Aizawl" or "Champhai". The app instantly loads that district's Village Councils, Category chips, Emergency services, and Government Offices!
  2. Test Multi-District Admin Portal Control:
    In the Admin app on the right (after entering your Admin PIN), use the "District" dropdown in the top control bar. Switch between All Districts, Kolasib, Aizawl, Champhai, etc. Notice that directory counts, contact lists, emergency helplines, offices, and citizen error reports immediately filter to that district.
  3. Test Adding & Pushing District-Specific Records:
    In the Admin app, click "+ Add Contact" or "+ Add Office". Select a district (e.g. Aizawl or Kolasib). Submit the form. Notice that if the Phonebook app on the left is viewing that district, the update appears instantaneously via SSE!
  4. Test District-Targeted Announcements:
    In the Admin app, go to Broadcast & App tab. Select "Target District: Kolasib" or "All Districts (State-wide)". Submit an alert. Only phonebooks matching the target district (or state-wide) receive the banner notification!
  5. Test Adding & Removing Village / Local Councils:
    In the Admin app, switch to the "Councils" tab. Click "+ Add Council", choose type (e.g. "Local Council (LC)" or "Village Council (VC)"), district and name. Click Add. In the Phonebook app on the left, tap the "Councils" tab to see the council immediately appear! Now in Admin, click "Remove Council" to delete it in real-time.
  6. Test Updating Developer Name & Email:
    In the Admin app, go to "Broadcast & App" tab and find the Developer & App Support Details section. Change the Developer Name and Email (e.g. Pu Lalhmangaiha / support@mizoram.gov.in) and click "Save & Push Developer Info". In the Phonebook app on the left, tap the "About" tab: the Developer Name and clickable Email are updated live!
  7. Test Google Sheets Export & Import:
    In the Admin app on the right, click the green "Google Sheets" button in the top header (or click "Sheets" on Directory, Emergency, or Offices).
    • Export: Click "Download Google Sheet CSV" or "Copy CSV" to get data formatted for Google Sheets.
    • Import: Switch to "Import from Google Sheet", choose "Paste Text" or "Sheet Link", and click "Start Import & Push Updates". Watch the Phonebook app on the left update immediately!
